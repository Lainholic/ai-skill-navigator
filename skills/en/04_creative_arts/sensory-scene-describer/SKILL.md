# Sensory Scene Describer — Immersive Olfactory and Spatial Description Generator

Transforms visual-heavy drafts into multi-sensory scenes using top, middle, and base note literary frameworks. It provides immediate vocabulary packs and evocative prose for authors, copywriters, and game narrative designers.

---

### 1. Operating Rules
- **Draft First**: Delivers a ready-to-use output draft immediately without introductory fluff or greetings.
- **Context Preservation**: Retains the core meaning and intent while trimming unnecessary noise.
- **Time Efficient**: Eliminates manual formatting and repetitive drafting to save you time.

---

### 2. Output Template
```markdown
### Scene: [Setting Name / Concept]
- **Atmospheric Palette**: [3-4 evocative sensory adjectives]

#### Note Breakdown
- **Top Note (Immediate Impression)**: [Sharp, immediate scents; light, transient textures]
- **Middle Note (Core Character)**: [Heart scents, temperature, ongoing environmental actions]
- **Base Note (Underlying Resonance)**: [Deep, lingering aromas; foundational textures, moisture/dryness]

#### Prose Sample
> "[1-2 immersive paragraphs synthesizing the notes into concrete narrative action.]"

#### Sensory Lexicon
- **Nouns**: [4 concrete olfactory/spatial nouns]
- **Verbs**: [4 sensory-rich environmental verbs]
- **Sensory Modifiers**: [4 tactile/olfactory adjectives]
```

---

### 3. User Notes
- The output is a working draft designed for quick review and adaptation.

---

### 🛠️ Quick Customization Tips
- **Increase Decay/Antiquity**: "Shift base notes toward damp paper, oxidized brass, and cellar dust."
- **Commercial Copy Focus**: "Adapt the prose sample into a 3-sentence lifestyle product introduction."
- **Gothic Tension**: "Reframe the scent profile to emphasize cold stone, stale wax, and incoming storm air."
