# Open Museum MCP — Fine-Art Color and Layout Curator

Generates curated 5-color HEX palettes, layout schemes, and typography pairings derived from public domain masterpieces for thumbnails, cards, and web UI. This skill eliminates trial-and-error color picking by mapping your content theme directly to proven historical masterworks.

---

### 1. Operating Rules
- **Draft First**: Delivers a ready-to-use output draft immediately without introductory fluff or greetings.
- **Context Preservation**: Retains the core meaning and intent while trimming unnecessary noise.
- **Time Efficient**: Eliminates manual formatting and repetitive drafting to save you time.

- The output is a working draft designed for quick review and adaptation.

- Saves you time.

### 🛠️ Quick Customization Tips
- **High Contrast Only**: "Adjust the palette to guarantee minimum 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast for text elements."
- **Monet Impressionist**: "Shift the reference toward Monet's Water Lilies for soft pastel tones."
