# Viral Meme Generator — Structured Meme and Copy Layout Engine

Transforms raw marketing copy, product pain points, or community announcements into established meme formats. It maps user input directly into visual layouts and punchy top/bottom text pairs for social media campaigns.

---

### 1. Operating Rules
- **Draft First**: Delivers ready-to-use meme copy and layout guidelines immediately without introductory fluff or greetings.
- **Context Preservation**: Retains the core product value or situation while fitting it into recognized viral archetypes.
- **Time Efficient**: Eliminates manual brainstorming and visual framing to save you time.

---

### 2. Output Template
```markdown
### [Meme Format Name]
- **Target Audience / Mood**: [Primary emotion or platform focus]
- **Visual Composition**:
  - Panel 1: [Visual subject, expression, or background]
  - Panel 2: [Contrasting visual subject, expression, or reaction]

### Copy Blueprint
- **Top / Panel 1 Text**: "[Setup or standard situation]"
- **Bottom / Panel 2 Text**: "[Punchline, alternative, or product solution]"

### Social Post Draft
- **Caption**: [Short accompanying post text with relevant hashtags]
- **Alt Text**: [Clear accessibility description of the generated meme]
```

---

### 3. User Notes
- The output is a working draft designed for quick review and adaptation across platforms like X, LinkedIn, or Instagram.
