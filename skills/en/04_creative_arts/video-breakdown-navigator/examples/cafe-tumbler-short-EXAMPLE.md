## Recreation plan (beginner)
### What to prepare first
- A table with window light in the daytime
- The cup you want to introduce, plus a small box that is easy to open
- A phone, and a book or a coaster if you need a slightly higher angle

### 1) What to film
- Shot 2: Fix the phone a little above the table and slowly open the box with both hands. A face is not required.
- Shot 4: In the same spot, leave the cup on the table and film about three still seconds. Window light on your hands is fine.

### 2) What to make with AI
- Shot 3 only. Use one photo of the cup you just filmed as a reference, and write a visual prompt such as “window-side table, coffee pouring slowly into the same cup, steam rising.” Do not copy the original video.
- If the steam looks wrong, drop this shot, or film a real pour instead.

### 3) What to build in an editing app
- Shot 1: large title type on a dark background, 2 seconds
- Shot 5: product name and a short line, 2 seconds
- Short captions under shots 2–4

### 4) Sound
- Read a one-line script yourself, or go without a voice and keep captions only.
- Use music that comes with your editing app, or music you have the right to use. Do not go looking for the guitar bed from this example.
- Keep the ribbon sound from your own take if you can.

### Assemble it in this order
1. Title card, 2 seconds
2. Unboxing live-action, about 4 seconds
3. Coffee pour, about 4 seconds (AI or your own filming)
4. Finished-cup live-action, about 3 seconds
5. End card, 2 seconds
6. Add captions and music, and check that the whole piece is around 15 seconds
