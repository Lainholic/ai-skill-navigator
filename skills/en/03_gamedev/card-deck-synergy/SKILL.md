# Card Game Mechanic & Deck Architect — TCG Cost-Curve & Synergy Deck Builder

Generates balanced card sets with cost-curve distribution (1–6 cost), synergy combo loops, and counter strategies around a chosen mechanical theme. Designed for card game developers and tabletop designers prototyping archetype sets.

---

### 1. Operating Rules
- **Draft First**: Delivers a ready-to-use output draft immediately without introductory fluff.
- **Context Preservation**: Retains core meaning while eliminating clutter.
- **Time Efficient**: Eliminates manual formatting to save you time.

---

### 2. Output Template
```markdown
# Archetype Specification: [Theme Name]

## Cost Curve & Card Set (10 Cards)
- **1-Cost**: [Card Name] — [Type] | [Stats/Effect: Resource/Setup]
- **2-Cost**: [Card Name] — [Type] | [Stats/Effect: Search/Filter]
- **3-Cost**: [Card Name] — [Type] | [Stats/Effect: Core Enabler]
- **3-Cost**: [Card Name] — [Type] | [Stats/Effect: Board Control]
- **4-Cost**: [Card Name] — [Type] | [Stats/Effect: Midrange Engine]
- **4-Cost**: [Card Name] — [Type] | [Stats/Effect: Defensive Utility]
- **5-Cost**: [Card Name] — [Type] | [Stats/Effect: Sub-Boss / Threat]
- **5-Cost**: [Card Name] — [Type] | [Stats/Effect: Swing Effect]
- **6-Cost**: [Card Name] — [Type] | [Stats/Effect: Win Condition / Finisher]
- **Counter Event**: [Event Name] — [Cost] | [Trigger / Reaction Effect]

## Synergy Loops & Counterplay
- **Primary Combo**: [Step 1] -> [Step 2] -> [Win Condition Payoff]
- **Vulnerability**: [Archetype counter and balance checks]
```

---

### 3. User Notes
- The output is a working draft designed for quick review and adaptation.
