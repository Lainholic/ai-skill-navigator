# Theme Factory — Visual Identity and Palette Generator

Converts thematic keywords into production-ready color schemes and typographic pairings for Notion pages, presentation slide decks, and digital cards. Eliminates visual trial-and-error by providing structured HEX values and contrast guidance.

---

### 1. Operating Rules
- **Draft First**: Delivers a complete color palette and hierarchy guide immediately without introductory fluff.
- **Contrast Compliance**: Ensures background, body text, and accent pairings satisfy basic readability standards.
- **Structured Output**: Uses exact 5-slot HEX tables, emoji swatches, and explicit background/accent assignments.
- **Time Efficient**: Eliminates manual color hunting across external websites to save you time.

---

```markdown

### Typography & Application Guide
```
